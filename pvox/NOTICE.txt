Package identity and testing
  The package identity is FM-1_7869 (tools/build.py FM1_IDENTITY). The update loader keeps the Felucca
  loader's own name, ota-FM-1_900 (firmware/loader/loader.c), as Felucca, Dinghy and X0X do.
  The Drums12-Transpose firmware was tested by Hans on a real FM-1 as FM-1_900. The FM-1_7869 package,
  with the loader that accepts both flash chips (0x856014 and 0xB36014), was hardware-tested by
  Hans on his own real FM-1 on Nov 22, 2026.
